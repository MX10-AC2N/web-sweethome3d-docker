Tips for the best experience

On desktop: works like any VNC web client. Mouse + keyboard are fully supported.
On phone/tablet: the remote desktop auto-resizes to fill your screen. Pinch-zoom is disabled to keep the VNC view 1:1 with your taps.
To scroll a long menu: tap the screen, then drag with one finger.
Right-click on touch devices: long-press (about half a second) on the canvas.
For very small phones (under 5"): consider rotating to landscape mode.

How it works: the full Sweet Home 3D desktop app runs inside this container. What you see in the browser is a live, hardware-accelerated stream of that desktop, rendered at the resolution of your device.
